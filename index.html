Why Choose Online Stamp Doc?

Most online stamp tools require you to upload your sensitive documents to a remote server — creating serious privacy risks for confidential business files. Online Stamp Doc processes everything locally in your browser, so your invoices, contracts, and confidential documents stay completely private. It's faster, safer, and works even offline once the page is loaded.

Quick Start Guide

1

Pick a Stamp Template or Shape

Start with a Quick Template (PAID, APPROVED, etc.) for instant results, or choose a shape and customize it fully from scratch.

2

Customize Color, Text & Effects

Set your ink color, enter your custom text, adjust font size, add a distressed effect, or upload your company logo to the center.

3

Download Stamp PNG or Stamp a Document

Click Download PNG to save just the stamp image, or click Confirm & Proceed to apply it to a PDF, JPG, or PNG document.

4

Upload, Position & Export

Upload your document, then drag the stamp to position it. Use the rotate handle or toolbar buttons to rotate. Click Export Stamped Document when done.

Pro Tip: Save Your Stamp Design

Use the Save / Load Presets section to save your stamp design for future visits — no account required.
