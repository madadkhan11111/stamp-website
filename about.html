About Online Stamp Doc

Our Vision

At Online Stamp Doc, we believe that the digital transformation of businesses should be accessible, affordable, and secure. For decades, physical rubber stamps have been a staple of professional life—representing authority, organization, and validation. As the world moves toward paperless offices, we recognized the need for a digital equivalent that doesn't compromise on the "official" feel of traditional documents.

Our mission is to provide the world's most intuitive and powerful online stamp creation tool, empowering freelancers, small businesses, and large enterprises to maintain professional standards without the need for physical hardware or expensive software subscriptions.

What Sets Us Apart?

Unlike many other online document tools, Online Stamp Doc was built with a "Privacy-First" architecture. Most PDF tools require you to upload your sensitive documents to their servers for processing. This creates a significant security risk for confidential contracts, financial statements, and personal IDs.

We do things differently. All the heavy lifting—rendering the stamps, modifying the PDF structures, and exporting the final files—happens entirely within your own web browser. Your files never touch our servers. This means you can use our tool even for your most sensitive documents with 100% confidence in your data privacy.

Our Technology Stack

To achieve high-performance document processing in the browser, we utilize industry-leading open-source libraries:

  • PDF.js: Developed by Mozilla, this library allows us to render PDF documents with pixel-perfect accuracy directly onto an HTML5 canvas.
  • PDF-lib: This powerful engine allows us to modify existing PDF files, enabling us to "burn" your custom stamps into the document layer rather than just placing an image on top.
  • HTML5 Canvas: Our stamp designer uses the latest web canvas technologies to provide a real-time, high-resolution preview of your design, including complex effects like transparency and distress textures.

Commitment to Free Access

We are committed to keeping the core features of Online Stamp Doc free for everyone. To support hosting and ongoing development, we display ads through Google AdSense. Advertisements never receive your documents — files stay in your browser. You can accept personalized ads or choose essential cookies only. See our Privacy Policy.

By using Online Stamp Doc, you are joining a community of thousands of professionals who value efficiency, security, and professional presentation. We are constantly updating our tool with new stamp shapes, fonts, and features based on user feedback.
